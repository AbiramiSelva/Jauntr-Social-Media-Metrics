                  How this is worked out. Series posts are compared two ways: against your other posts in the same dates (is the series pulling its weight?), and against the typical series post (which topics, openings, formats and days do best). Patterns need at least 2–3 posts behind them, and anything under 6 series posts is an early signal, not proof.

                            Files on the dashboard

                            What your file needs

                            A date for each postColumns like Publish time, Date, Posted or Create time. Times are used for the best-time-to-post insight.
                            Some numbersViews, Plays, Impressions or Reach, plus Likes or Reactions, Comments, Shares, Saves or Favorites, and Follows if you have them.
                            Nice to haveCaption or Description, Permalink or Link, Post type (Reel, Carousel, Video), Post ID, and a Series or Tag column.
                            PlatformWorked out from a Platform column, the file name (e.g. tiktok-oct.csv) or post links. If it can't tell, you pick it before saving.
                            Duplicates are fineThe same post is matched by its ID or link, so uploading it twice never counts it twice. When two files disagree, the one with the higher (more up-to-date) numbers is used.
                            PinterestUse Analytics → Overview → Export in Pinterest. The export only includes the metric picked in the chart, so export once with Impressions and once with Pin clicks (same dates) to get both. Uploading a newer export updates the overlapping days.
                            Video numbersIf the export has Duration, Average watch time or Watched full video columns, “What's working” also looks at video length and how long people watch.
                            Audience by countryThe countries or locations breakdown from TikTok, Instagram or Facebook. It shows how much of each audience is in the US and Sri Lanka.
                            Not usedOther account reports (followers by day, viewers, active hours) aren't used. PDFs and screenshots can't be read, so export the CSV or Excel instead.
