          What your file needs

          A date for each postColumns like Publish time, Date, Posted or Create time. Times are used for the best-time-to-post insight.
          Some numbersViews, Plays, Impressions or Reach, plus Likes or Reactions, Comments, Shares, Saves or Favorites, and Follows if you have them.
          Nice to haveCaption or Description, Permalink or Link, Post type (Reel, Carousel, Video), Post ID, and a Series or Tag column.
          PlatformWorked out from a Platform column, the file name (e.g. tiktok-oct.csv) or post links. If it can't tell, you pick it before saving.
          Duplicates are fineThe same post is matched by its ID or link, so uploading it twice never counts it twice. When two files disagree, the one with the higher (more up-to-date) numbers is used.
          Account and audience reportsFiles with one row per day (followers, viewers, account overview), breakdowns (gender, countries) and active hours go to the Account & audience tab.
          Anything elseTables it doesn't recognise are still kept and shown as a table on the Account & audience tab. PDFs and screenshots can't be read, so export the CSV or Excel instead.
